From the Manhattan to the Old-Fashioned to the Sazerac, rye whiskey is an essential ingredient in many of the world’s most famous whiskey cocktails. But for decades, it was incredibly hard to find the spirit in most liquor stores and bars. One of the greatest untold spirits stories is the launch of Rittenhouse Straight Rye Whisky in New York’s bars and how this bottled-in-bond liquor helped kick off the rebirth of the craft cocktail around the world.
