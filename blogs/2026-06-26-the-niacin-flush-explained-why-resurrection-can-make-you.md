If you experience warmth, redness, tingling, or a sudden flush after taking Resurrection, don't worry—it's a normal response to the active Niacin (Vitamin B3) in the formula. Known as a Niacin Flush, this temporary effect is caused by increased blood flow near the skin's surface and typically appears within 10–20 minutes before fading on its own. The intensity varies from person to person depending on factors like alcohol intake, food, hydration, and individual sensitivity.
