Spiced Rum is not your typical mixing rum. Unlike the lighter styles used in something like a Piña Colada, Spiced Rum infuses with warming spices such as cinnamon, vanilla, pepper, and nutmeg, often layered with hints of citrus or ginger. The result is a rum with depth, character, and just the right amount of kick to make things interesting.
