Few spirits categories are as rooted in tradition and a sense of place as the Mexican agave-spirit, mezcal. For Montelobos, the desire to combine Oaxacan tradition with cutting-edge experimentation has resulted in an array of innovative spirits and techniques—none more so, however, than its pechuga mezcal, which is made with organically cultivated agave and is certified kosher.
